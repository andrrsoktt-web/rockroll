FAQ

How do I throw? Drag your finger back anywhere on the screen and release. A longer pull means a stronger throw.

How do I unlock the next league? Beat the boss on track 20 of the current league.

How do I change the language? Tap the globe button on the title screen.

I lost my progress. Progress is stored on the device; deleting the app removes it.

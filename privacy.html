Privacy Policy

Rock Roll · last updated 19 November 2026

Rock Roll is published by FOP Olha Ursul, Ukraine (“we”). This policy explains what data the game uses.

Data we collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail or any account. Your game progress (stars, records, settings, language) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device model, OS version, and ad interaction data. If you deny tracking in the iOS prompt, ads are not personalized. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown and can change your choice at any time. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

Rock Roll is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

You can reset the advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Deleting the app removes all locally stored progress. For any request, write to us.
